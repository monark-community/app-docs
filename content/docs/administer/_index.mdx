# Administer

For admins: set up your organization so everyone else can get to work. Admins only; if you hold the Administrator role, **Admin** appears in the navigation rail. Setting up a new organization? Start with [Set up your organization](/docs/get-started/set-up-your-organization).

- [People and access](/docs/administer/people-and-access): bring people in, and decide what each can see and do.
- [Data models](/docs/administer/data-models): design your organization's databases, who can use them, and their public forms.
- [Integrations](/docs/administer/integrations): connect Monark to other systems with secrets, connected services, webhooks and service accounts.
- [Organization](/docs/administer/organization): organization-wide settings: name and color, files, achievements, audit trail.
