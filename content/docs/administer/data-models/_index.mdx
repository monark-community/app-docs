# Data models

Design your organization's databases: which models exist, what fields they hold, who can work in them, and how people outside the app can contribute. Your teammates then work with the records under **Data**; see [Data](/docs/use/data).

Admins only. If you hold the Administrator role, **Admin** appears at the bottom of the navigation rail (in the menu on a phone); these pages start at its **Data Models** tab.

- [Create a data model](/docs/administer/data-models/create-a-data-model): add a new database, or delete and restore one.
- [Add and change fields](/docs/administer/data-models/add-and-change-fields): shape what every record holds.
- [Give a role access to a model's records](/docs/administer/data-models/give-a-role-access-to-a-models-records): decide who can read, add, change and delete records.
- [Show a model's records on a calendar](/docs/administer/data-models/show-a-models-records-on-a-calendar): turn each record into a calendar event.
- [Publish a public form](/docs/administer/data-models/publish-a-public-form): let people without an account submit records through a link.
- [Moderate a public board](/docs/administer/data-models/moderate-a-public-board): approve submissions for a public list and manage votes and comments.
