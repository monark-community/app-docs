# Manage a user's account

Fix someone's profile, help them back in when they've lost their password, or adjust what they're notified about.

1. In **Admin**, open **Users**.
2. Find the person: search by name or email, or open the filter menu to narrow by **Role**, **Status**, **Joined** or **Email verified**.
3. Click the person. Their details open in a side panel; **Open full page** gives them the whole screen.

## Edit their profile

Under **Profile**, change their picture, banner, **Display name**, **Bio** or **Language**. Each change saves when you leave the field.

## Send a password reset

Under **Account actions**, click **Send password reset email**. They get the standard reset link and choose the new password themselves; you never see it. If they also lost their two-factor device and recovery codes, your operator follows [Recover a locked-out user](/docs/operate/recover-a-locked-out-user).

## Change their notifications

Under **Notification preferences**, switch categories on or off for in-app and email. Security emails can't be switched off. Most people prefer to manage this themselves.

Good to know:

- You can't change a user's email address. They change it themselves; see [Change your email or password](/docs/use/account/change-your-email-or-password).
- While a user's deletion is scheduled, their profile, roles and settings are locked.
