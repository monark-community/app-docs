# Concepts

How Monark works and why it is built that way. Read these to understand a behavior before you change it or explain it; the steps themselves are in [Build on Monark](/docs/build) and [Operate](/docs/operate).

- [Platform overview](/docs/concepts/platform-overview): what Monark is, what ships in every deploy, and how the pieces fit. Start here.
- [Architecture](/docs/concepts/architecture): modules, boundaries, events, generated code, boot order, the web shell and integrations.
- [Users and authentication](/docs/concepts/users-and-authentication): accounts, sign-in, two-factor, trusted devices and sessions.
- [Permissions](/docs/concepts/permissions): roles, permissions and record scopes, and how they combine.
- [Data models and queries](/docs/concepts/data-models-and-queries): how custom models store records and how a query becomes SQL.
- [How automations run](/docs/concepts/automations): triggers, the run queue, retries, and how data moves between steps.
- [Notifications](/docs/concepts/notifications): kinds, channels, preferences and how a notification is delivered.
- [Files](/docs/concepts/files): uploads, buckets, validation and download links.
- [Block editor](/docs/concepts/block-editor): how long-form content is stored and rendered.
- [How achievements are awarded](/docs/concepts/achievements): conditions, thresholds and the award worker.
- [Identity and integration](/docs/concepts/identity-and-integration): how API keys, service accounts, webhooks and secrets fit together.
- [Secrets](/docs/concepts/secrets): how integration credentials are stored, encrypted and kept write-only.
- [How the AI assistant works](/docs/concepts/chat-agent): a turn, where tools come from, and why every write waits for you.
