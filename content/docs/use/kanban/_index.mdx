# Kanban

Track work as cards on a board. A board has columns for each stage of the work, and you move cards across as work progresses. Open **Kanban** in the navigation rail; if it isn't there, Kanban isn't enabled for your organization.

What you can do depends on your role: viewing, creating boards, editing columns and cards, deleting, and managing who sees a board are separate permissions.

- [Create a board](/docs/use/kanban/create-a-board): start a new project or workflow, and choose who sees it.
- [Set up columns](/docs/use/kanban/set-up-columns): name the stages and limit how many cards each holds.
- [Add a card](/docs/use/kanban/add-a-card): capture a task with its owner, due date, priority and checklist.
- [Update a card](/docs/use/kanban/update-a-card): move it to the next stage, change its details, or delete it.
- [Find cards](/docs/use/kanban/find-cards): filter a board, save a view, or search every board.
