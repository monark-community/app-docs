# Your account

Sign in, keep your account secure, and decide how Monark reaches you. To open your account settings, click your avatar at the top right, open the **Account** tab, and choose **Profile**, **Account & Security** or **Notifications**.

- [Create an account](/docs/use/account/create-an-account): sign up with your email address.
- [Sign in](/docs/use/account/sign-in): get into the app with your email, a connected provider, or a two-factor code.
- [Reset your password](/docs/use/account/reset-your-password): get back in when you've forgotten it.
- [Edit your profile](/docs/use/account/edit-your-profile): set your name, picture, language and theme.
- [Read your notifications](/docs/use/account/read-your-notifications): see what happened and clear what you've dealt with.
- [Choose your email notifications](/docs/use/account/choose-your-email-notifications): decide which events also email you.
- [Turn on two-factor authentication](/docs/use/account/turn-on-two-factor-authentication): require a code from your phone at sign-in.
- [Change your email or password](/docs/use/account/change-your-email-or-password): update how you sign in.
- [Connect a sign-in provider](/docs/use/account/connect-a-sign-in-provider): sign in with an outside account as well as, or instead of, a password.
- [Sign out other devices](/docs/use/account/sign-out-other-devices): see where you're signed in and end sessions you don't recognise.
- [Create an API key](/docs/use/account/create-an-api-key): let a script or integration act as you.
- [Delete your account](/docs/use/account/delete-your-account): schedule your account for deletion, or cancel it.
