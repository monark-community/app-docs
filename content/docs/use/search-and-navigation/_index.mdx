# Search and navigation

Get to the part of the app you need, or straight to a record, card or page by name. You only see the areas your organization has turned on and your role can reach.

- [Move around the app](/docs/use/search-and-navigation/move-around-the-app): use the navigation rail, the phone menu, the breadcrumb and section tabs.
- [Search across the app](/docs/use/search-and-navigation/search-across-the-app): jump to any record, card, event, page or automation by name.

Every shortcut is listed in [Keyboard shortcuts](/docs/reference/keyboard-shortcuts).
