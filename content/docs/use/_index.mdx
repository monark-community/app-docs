# Use Monark

How to get your everyday work done in the app, one page per task. New here? [Take your first steps](/docs/get-started/first-steps). You only see the areas your organization uses; if one is missing from your navigation, ask your admin.

- [Your account](/docs/use/account): sign in, keep your account secure, and decide how Monark reaches you.
- [Search and navigation](/docs/use/search-and-navigation): get to an area, or straight to a record, card or page by name.
- [Data](/docs/use/data): find, add and keep track of records in your organization's databases.
- [Kanban](/docs/use/kanban): plan and track work as cards on a board.
- [Calendar](/docs/use/calendar): plan events on shared, color-coded calendars.
- [Wiki](/docs/use/wiki): write and organize your organization's shared pages.
- [Automations](/docs/use/automations): draw flows that run by themselves when something happens.
- [Assistant](/docs/use/assistant): ask questions about your data and approve the changes it makes.
- [See your achievements](/docs/use/achievements): the badges you've earned and the ones still to earn.
